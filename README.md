# LVMH Revenue vs Macro Inflation: Quantitative Analysis (2018–2024)

## What is the project about?
An Excel-based analysis of LVMH's segment revenues against CPI inflation 
data for France, USA, China, and Japan, covering 2018–2024.

## Key findings
- Fashion & Leather Goods grew at a 14.3% CAGR, from €18.5bn to €41.1bn
- LVMH outpaced French inflation in 5 of 6 years studied
- Real (inflation-adjusted) revenue still grew substantially, confirming 
  genuine volume and pricing power gains, not just nominal inflation effects
- China's near-zero inflation in 2023–24 reveals its demand slowdown was 
  a confidence problem, not a price problem

## Data sources
- LVMH Annual Reports 2018–2024
- World Bank CPI Data (indicator: FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG)

## Tools used
- Microsoft Excel
